Hailey Bieber's Morning Rituals & Rhode Skincare

Model and entrepreneur Hailey Bieber is famous for her aesthetic lifestyle and radiant skin. Many people want to know how she starts her day to look so fresh and organized.

Hailey usually wakes up early at around 7:30 AM. First, she drinks a large glass of warm water to hydrate her body. Next, she begins her signature skincare routine. She applies hydrogel eye patches to gently soothe her face and reduce morning puffiness.

After washing her face, she applies glaze fluid and moisturizer from her beauty brand, Rhode. She believes that gentleness is key: she smoothly massages products into her skin to create a natural glow.

When working from home, Hailey prefers wearing a cozy lounge set or knit outfit. While taking time for herself, she listens to calm music, drinks coffee, or writes in her daily notebook. For Hailey, mornings are not rushed; they are a peaceful ritual to prepare for a successful day.

Part 5 • 10 Minutes

Grammar: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Master routines (Present Simple) vs. actions happening right now (Present Continuous).

Present Simple

Daily Habits & Routines

Use for repeated daily actions, habits, or general truths.

Signal words: usually, every day, always, often, normally

"I drink warm water every morning."

"She applies eye patches every day."

Present Continuous

Current Actions

Use for actions happening right now at the moment of speaking (am/is/are + verb-ing).

Signal words: now, right now, at the moment, look!

"Right now, she is reading a book in the bath."

"Look! She is wearing a cozy cardigan."

Grammar Exercises

Choose the correct verb tense form for each sentence:

4. At the moment, she (relax) in a hot bath with a book.
